1. Program Name

Servpro Emergency Notifications (Operated by Merrimack AI & Data Solutions)

2. Program Description

The Servpro Emergency Notifications program, operated by Merrimack AI & Data Solutions, sends operational SMS text messages to Servpro team members, employees, and on-call staff when emergency service calls are received. Notifications include caller information, incident details, and property location to enable rapid response to water damage, fire damage, mold, storm damage, and other property emergencies.

3. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on incoming emergency call volume. Typical frequency is 0 to 10 messages per day. Messages are sent only when an emergency call is received and routed to your franchise.

4. Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. Standard messaging rates from your wireless carrier apply to all SMS messages sent and received through this program. Contact your carrier for details about your messaging plan.

5. Opt-In

Participation in this SMS program requires explicit consent. Team members opt in by signing the Internal Communications Consent Form during onboarding or employment updates. By signing, you consent to receive operational SMS notifications at the mobile number provided.

6. Opt-Out

You can opt out of SMS notifications at any time:

  • Reply STOP to any message to immediately unsubscribe
  • Contact your franchise manager to be removed from the notification list

After opting out, you will receive a one-time confirmation message. No further messages will be sent unless you re-enroll.
