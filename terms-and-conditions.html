Servpro Emergency Notifications — SMS Program

Last Updated: April 4, 2026

1. Program Name

Servpro Emergency Notifications

2. Program Description

The Servpro Emergency Notifications program sends operational SMS text messages to Servpro team members, employees, and on-call staff when emergency service calls are received. Notifications include caller information, incident details, and property location to enable rapid response to water damage, fire damage, mold, storm damage, and other property emergencies.

3. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on incoming emergency call volume. Typical frequency is 0 to 10 messages per day. Messages are sent only when an emergency call is received and routed to your franchise.

4. Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. Standard messaging rates from your wireless carrier apply to all SMS messages sent and received through this program. Contact your carrier for details about your messaging plan.

5. Opt-In

Participation in this SMS program is part of your role as a Servpro team member. You are enrolled when added to the on-call notification schedule by your franchise manager. By accepting your on-call assignment, you consent to receive operational SMS notifications at the mobile number provided.

6. Opt-Out

You can opt out of SMS notifications at any time:

  • Reply STOP to any message to immediately unsubscribe
  • Contact your franchise manager to be removed from the notification list

After opting out, you will receive a one-time confirmation message. No further messages will be sent unless you re-enroll.

7. Help

For support with this SMS program:

  • Reply HELP to any message for assistance
  • Email: support@servpro-emergency.com

8. Supported Carriers

This program is compatible with all major US wireless carriers including AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, Sprint, and others. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
