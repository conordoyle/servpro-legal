Privacy Policy

Servpro Emergency Notifications

Last Updated: April 4, 2026

1. Overview

This privacy policy describes how Servpro ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our internal Emergency Notification SMS program. This program sends operational text message alerts to Servpro team members, employees, and contractors ("Recipients") regarding incoming emergency service calls.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following information from Recipients:

  • Name — to identify team members in our on-call scheduling system
  • Mobile phone number — to deliver SMS notifications
  • Email address — to deliver email notifications
  • Role and franchise assignment — to route notifications to the appropriate team

3. How We Use Your Information

Recipient information is used exclusively to:

  • Send operational SMS and email alerts when emergency calls are received
  • Manage on-call scheduling and notification routing
  • Ensure the right team members are contacted for the right franchise location

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share Recipient personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Recipient data is shared only with:

  • Twilio — our SMS delivery provider, solely for the purpose of transmitting text messages
  • Resend — our email delivery provider, solely for the purpose of transmitting email notifications

These service providers are contractually obligated to use Recipient data only for delivering our messages and are prohibited from using it for any other purpose.

5. Data Retention

Recipient contact information is retained for as long as the individual is an active team member. Upon departure or removal from the on-call schedule, contact information is deleted from our notification systems.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect Recipient information, including encrypted data storage and secure API communications with our service providers.

7. Your Rights

Recipients may at any time:

  • Reply STOP to any SMS message to unsubscribe from text notifications
  • Reply HELP for support information
  • Request removal from the notification system through their franchise manager
  • Request access to or deletion of their personal data by contacting us

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Recipients will be notified of material changes.
